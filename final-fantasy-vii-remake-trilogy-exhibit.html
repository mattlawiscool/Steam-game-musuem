FILE 01 Origins Project History &
Timeline

First teased as a tech demo back in 2005, a full remake of 1997's Final Fantasy VII wasn't officially confirmed until 2015 — and when it finally arrived, Square Enix revealed something nobody expected: the story of Midgar alone would fill an entire game. What began as one project split into a three-part series, with Remake covering just the opening act of the original and Rebirth picking up the journey across the wider planet.

Both games follow Cloud Strife, a former Shinra SOLDIER turned mercenary, as he joins the eco-terrorist group AVALANCHE to fight the mako-draining Shinra Electric Power Company — and, eventually, to confront Sephiroth, the fallen hero shadowing Cloud's own fractured memory.

Project Timeline

  • 2015A full remake of Final Fantasy VII is officially announced, a decade after first being teased.
  • 2020Final Fantasy VII Remake launches April 10 on PS4, covering only Midgar — and becomes Square Enix's best-selling digital PlayStation release.
  • 2021Remake Intergrade arrives on PS5 with the Yuffie-focused INTERmission episode.
  • 2022Remake Intergrade comes to PC via Steam in December; Rebirth is officially confirmed as the trilogy's second chapter.
  • 2024Final Fantasy VII Rebirth launches February 29 on PS5 to widespread critical acclaim.
  • 2025Rebirth arrives on PC via Steam and Epic Games Store, January 23.
  • 2026Remake Intergrade reaches Switch 2 and Xbox; the trilogy's finale, Final Fantasy VII Revelation, is revealed for Spring 2027.
