FILE 01 Origins Case File &
Timeline

Persona 5 put a group of Tokyo high schoolers — the Phantom Thieves of Hearts — in the business of infiltrating the twisted "Palaces" of corrupt adults and stealing the distorted desires at their core. Its 2016 original launch turned Atlus's stylish turn-based RPG into a mainstream phenomenon. Persona 5 Royal, released in 2019 in Japan, is the definitive version: a new character, a new semester of story, new dungeons, and a reworked final act.

Royal has since been ported everywhere from Xbox to Switch to PC, turning what was once a PS4-exclusive cult favorite into one of Atlus's best-selling games ever.

Case Timeline

  • 2016Persona 5 launches in Japan on PS3/PS4, hitting the West in 2017.
  • 2019Persona 5 Royal releases in Japan Oct 31, adding Kasumi and a new third semester.
  • 2020Royal arrives in the West on PS4, March 31 — instantly one of the year's top-rated games.
  • 2022Ports to Xbox, PC, and Nintendo Switch in October, reaching its widest audience yet.
  • 2026Royal alone crosses 8.6 million copies sold; the wider Persona series tops 30 million.
