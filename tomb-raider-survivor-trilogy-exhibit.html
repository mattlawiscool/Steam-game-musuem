LOG 01 The Reboot Origins &
Trilogy Arc

After the Legend–Anniversary–Underworld era wound down, Crystal Dynamics didn't build a sequel — they rebuilt Lara Croft from the ground up. Rather than the acrobatic icon of the '90s, the reboot's Lara is a 21-year-old archaeology graduate, shipwrecked and utterly unprepared, who has to become a survivor before she can become a legend. That single decision — origin story over continuation — shaped everything that followed across all three games.

The trilogy was told across three chapters and, unusually, two different studios: Crystal Dynamics led the first two entries, while Eidos-Montréal took over for the finale, leaning the series further into exploration and puzzle-heavy tombs as Lara's arc reached its end.

Trilogy Timeline

  • 2013Tomb Raider reboots the series on Yamatai — Lara's first, brutal survival.
  • 2015Rise of the Tomb Raider sends her into Siberia, chasing her father's research.
  • 2018Shadow of the Tomb Raider closes the arc in Peru and Mexico, as she becomes the Tomb Raider.
  • 2021All three are bundled together as the Definitive Survivor Trilogy.
  • 2024An animated Netflix series, The Legend of Lara Croft, continues the character's story.
  • 2026New mainline Tomb Raider entries are in development, continuing beyond the reboot era.
