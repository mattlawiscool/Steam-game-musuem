PAGE 01 The Story So Far Origins &
Timeline

Alan Wake began as Remedy's genre experiment: a bestselling thriller writer arrives in the small town of Bright Falls only to have his wife vanish and his own unwritten manuscript start coming true around him, page by page. Thirteen years later, Alan Wake 2 picked the thread back up as Remedy's first full survival-horror game — splitting the story between Alan, still trapped writing his way out of an alternate dimension called the Dark Place, and Saga Anderson, an FBI agent investigating ritual murders that trace back to Bright Falls.

Both games sit inside what Remedy calls the Remedy Connected Universe — the same fictional world shared with Control, where Alan Wake's manuscript pages, Federal Bureau of Control case files, and Saga's investigation all quietly reference each other.
