SEC.01 Origins Case File &
Timeline

Cyberpunk 2077 was announced by CD Projekt Red in 2012, adapted from Mike Pondsmith's tabletop RPG Cyberpunk 2020. Its 2018 gameplay reveal built years of hype around Night City and a mercenary protagonist known only as V. Keanu Reeves joined the cast as Johnny Silverhand, a digitized rockerboy sharing space in V's own head.

The December 2020 launch was one of the most notorious in gaming history — a buggy, unfinished-feeling release, especially on last-gen consoles, led Sony to briefly pull the game from the PlayStation Store entirely. What followed was a multi-year comeback: years of patches, a hit Netflix anime tie-in, and a major expansion that turned Night City's reputation around.
