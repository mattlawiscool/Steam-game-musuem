ENTRY I Origins Chronicle &
Timeline

Adapted from Andrzej Sapkowski's Witcher novels, Wild Hunt closes out CD Projekt Red's trilogy following Geralt of Rivia, a mutated monster hunter searching for his adopted daughter Ciri as a spectral army — the Wild Hunt — closes in behind her. Built on the REDengine 3, it traded the earlier games' corridors for a sprawling, morally grey open world.

It launched to immediate acclaim in May 2015 and never really left the conversation — over a decade later, it's still regularly named among the greatest RPGs ever made, and continues to sell in the millions each year.

Chronicle

  • 2013Debut gameplay trailer shown at E3, followed by the "Killing Monsters" cinematic that August.
  • 2015Releases May 19 on PC, PS4 and Xbox One to widespread critical acclaim.
  • 2015Hearts of Stone, the first expansion, arrives in October.
  • 2016Blood and Wine, the second and final expansion, closes out Geralt's story in May.
  • 2019Ported to Nintendo Switch in October; the Netflix adaptation premieres in December.
  • 2022A free next-gen update lands on PS5 and Xbox Series in December.
  • 2026Surpasses 65 million copies sold; a third expansion, Songs of the Past, is announced.
