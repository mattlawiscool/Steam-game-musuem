FILE 01 The Collection Why These
Five

The X-Men have had one of gaming's strangest publishing histories: decades of beloved console and arcade titles that, thanks to shifting film and licensing deals, mostly never made it to PC — and several that did have since vanished from digital storefronts entirely. What's actually purchasable on Steam right now is a mix of arcade-era rereleases and modern titles that fold X-Men characters into a wider Marvel roster rather than headlining a game on their own.

This is a snapshot of that mix — from the genre-defining 1992 arcade brawler to the current live-service hero shooter — rather than a complete history of every X-Men game ever made.

A Rough Timeline

  • 1992X-Men: The Arcade Game defines the team beat-'em-up formula in arcades.
  • 1996X-Men vs. Street Fighter kicks off Capcom's mutant-fueled crossover fighting era.
  • 2013Marvel Puzzle Quest launches free-to-play, leaning hard on its X-Men roster from day one.
  • 2022Marvel's Midnight Suns folds Wolverine and Magik into a tactical, card-driven squad.
  • 2024Marvel vs. Capcom Fighting Collection and Marvel Rivals both arrive, reviving the arcade classics and the roster alike.
  • 2026MARVEL MaXimum Collection brings the original 1992 X-Men arcade cabinet back with online play.
