LEDGER 01 Origins Case File &
Timeline

Set twelve years before the original Red Dead Redemption, RDR2 casts players as Arthur Morgan, a senior enforcer in Dutch van der Linde's outlaw gang. As federal agents and bounty hunters close in and the frontier itself gives way to the modern age, Arthur is forced to choose between loyalty to the gang that raised him and his own fraying conscience.

Rockstar's follow-up to the original 2010 game became one of the most acclaimed releases in the medium's history — winning over 175 Game of the Year awards and, remarkably, continuing to sell millions of copies a year even without a current-gen console version.

LEDGER 04 Guides & Tips Outlaw Notes
01Don't rush Chapter 2 — it's the game's soft tutorial. Hunt, fish, and explore Horseshoe Overlook before the story picks up pace.
02Loot everything: dressers, corpses, cabinets. Stolen jewelry doesn't sell at general stores — look for Fences in Emerald Ranch, Saint Denis, and Van Horn.
03Honor shapes more than dialogue — it gates certain missions and outcomes entirely, so decide early whether you're playing a righteous outlaw or a ruthless one.
04Bond with your horse regularly; a fully bonded mount is calmer in combat, faster to recover stamina, and far less likely to throw you.
