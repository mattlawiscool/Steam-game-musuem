FILE 01 Origins Incident Log &
Timeline

Jesse Faden walks into the Federal Bureau of Control's headquarters — the Oldest House, a shape-shifting brutalist monolith hidden in plain sight in New York — looking for answers about her missing brother. Within minutes, she's named the Bureau's new Director, tasked with fighting back an invasive force called the Hiss that has possessed nearly everyone inside.

Remedy built Control around the SCP Foundation-style idea of "Objects of Power" — everyday items warped into reality-bending artifacts — and paired it with a shifting, Brutalist-architecture setting that became the game's defining signature.

FILE 04 Guides & Tips Field Procedures
01Prioritize the Launch ability early — throwing debris at enemies is often faster and safer than gunfights, especially against groups.
02Seek out Control Points as soon as you spot them; they're fast travel, upgrade stations, and free health refills all in one.
03Read the found documents even when they seem tangential — they gate several achievements and make the Bureau's bureaucratic horror land much harder.
04Bring a controller if you can — the aiming and dash-heavy combat were tuned first for gamepad, even on PC.
