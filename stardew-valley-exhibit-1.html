GALLERY I Origins Provenance &
Timeline

Stardew Valley was built almost entirely by one person: Eric Barone, working under the name ConcernedApe. Over roughly four years, he taught himself programming, pixel art, and music composition from scratch to build the game alone, drawing inspiration from classic farming sims like Harvest Moon. Partway through development, the British studio Chucklefish offered to publish the game, freeing Barone to focus on finishing it.

What started as a niche homage to an older genre became one of the best-selling independent games ever made — credited with sparking a full revival of the farm-sim genre.
